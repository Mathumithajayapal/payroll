Milestones & Growth

Our Journey: From an Idea to Industry Leadership

A timeline of relentless innovation, scaling from a local startup to a trusted nationwide compensation platform.

2021 • Inception

The Seed of Innovation

Founded by HR and fintech veterans frustrated by manual calculation errors, spreadsheet chaos, and legal penalties that plagued growing companies. Built our first MVP with automated gross-to-net calculations.

Key Breakthrough
First 50 beta companies onboarded with 100% error-free payroll trials.
2022 • Expansion

Direct Banking Integrations

Partnered with premier commercial banks to introduce one-click batch salary disbursement via secure NEFT/RTGS rails, cutting bank upload delays to zero.

Milestone Achieved
Crossed 500+ active business customers and automated ₹100M+ in disbursements.
2024 • Technology Leap

Mobile ESS & AI Auditing

Launched the Employee Self-Service (ESS) mobile app and pre-flight AI audit engine to flag anomalies, missing tax declarations, and attendance discrepancies before payout.

Product Metric
Over 75,000 employees active on the self-service mobile portal monthly.
2026 & Beyond • Leadership

Pan-India Enterprise Scale

Surpassed 2,500 enterprise customers, processing ₹50M+ monthly across 18 states while retaining SOC-2 Type II, ISO 27001, and 100% compliance records.

Current Status
Ranked #1 automated payroll software for high-growth businesses.
